07 About the restaurant

First we farmed, then we cooked

Bela reka has been in New Belgrade since 2017. We didn't start with a menu, we started with a farm - first we grew the produce, then we built a restaurant around it.

The cooking is traditional, our staff welcome you like a guest in their own home, and the dishes look modern without losing the taste of a home-cooked meal.
